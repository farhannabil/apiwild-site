- Inference gateway: streaming, model discovery, routing/fallbacks, budgets, quotas, rate limits, caching and retry policy.
- Token metering, usage dashboards, cost ledger, invoices, credits, billing webhooks and order provisioning.
- Model comparison with actual benchmarks/latency, playground/chat, rankings, modality-specific APIs.
- Durable custom brief submission, support tickets and customer communications.
- Autonomous agents, workflows, integrations, execution permissions, approvals, audit logs and incident handling.
- Fine-tuning, evaluations, batch inference and dedicated deployments if adopted as product scope.
- Verified security, regional processing, retention rules, enterprise SSO and production terms/privacy/AUP.
- Monitoring and measured service health; no fabricated uptime/customer logos/compliance claims.

All production items remain open. No provider contracts, credentials, billing or runtime agents were supplied or activated during this redesign.
